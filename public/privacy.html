Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

DeLacroix is operated by Rynos LLC (“we,” “us”). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Your account: email address, display name, and password (stored as a secure hash).
  • Contacts you add: the name, email address, phone number, birthday, and relationship of people you choose to send gifts to. You are responsible for having their permission to share this information with us.
  • Gifts: the messages you write, occasions you choose, and any cash gift amounts, along with delivery and claim activity (sent, claimed, declined dates).
  • Payments (when enabled): payment card details are collected and processed by Stripe; we never see or store full card numbers.

How we use it

  • To deliver gifts: we send recipients a one-time text message or email, on your behalf, containing your name and a link to claim the gift you sent. We do not send recipients marketing.
  • To operate your account, show your gift history, and provide support.
  • To keep the service safe, prevent abuse, and comply with law.

We do not sell your information. We do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

Only service providers that make DeLacroix work: Supabase (database and authentication), Twilio (text-message delivery), Resend (email delivery), Stripe (payments), and Expo (app infrastructure). Each receives only what it needs to perform its function.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account at any time in the app (You → Delete Account). This permanently removes your account, your contacts, and gifts you have sent. Gifts you claimed from others remain with their senders, unlinked from your account.

Recipients

If someone sent you a gift through DeLacroix, we hold the contact details they provided about you solely to deliver that gift. To have those details removed, contact us at the address below.

Children

DeLacroix is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy here and update the date above.
