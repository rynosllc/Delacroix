How We Use Your Information

DeLacroix is a luxury gifting platform. When you send a gift through our app, the recipient receives a one-time SMS notification containing a link to view and claim their gift. Recipients can reply STOP at any time to opt out of future messages from DeLacroix.

We collect only the information necessary to deliver your gift. Recipient phone numbers are provided by senders and used solely for gift delivery notifications. We never sell or share personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

For questions about your privacy or to request data deletion, contact us at: ismael@rynosops.com
